Summary

Tomb Raider: The Prophecy is an honest attempt to condense the franchise's core formula into a handheld format. Given the GBA's hardware limitations, the title performs well, mainly thanks to the isometric perspective that competently simulates a 3D environment. However, the experience doesn't go much deeper than that. It is a solid pastime for a quick session, but ultimately not very memorable.
