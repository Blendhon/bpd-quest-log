Is it worth playing? Absolutely. The core game was already deeply satisfying, and this remake maintains that exact standard with a fresh coat of paint.

Is it worth buying at full price, though? No. As good as the visual and structural polish is, it is still fundamentally the same game from two decades ago. Paying full price for a nostalgic visual upgrade isn't justified.
