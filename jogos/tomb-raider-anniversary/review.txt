--- Summary ---
Tomb Raider: Anniversary is a worthy remake of the original classic, adding fresh areas, mechanics, and narrative layers to the franchise.

However, it feels like there was some lost potential: certain iconic areas were removed, challenges were simplified, and combat was largely reduced to Quick Time Events (QTEs). I also encountered some performance stutters—only about 4 or 5 instances, but they were noticeable enough to mention.

Overall, it's a solid reimagining, even if it sacrifices some of the original's complexity.

--- Resumo ---
Tomb Raider: Anniversary é um remake digno do título original, trazendo novas áreas, mecânicas e camadas à franquia.

Por outro lado, há um sentimento de potencial desperdiçado: algumas áreas foram removidas, desafios foram simplificados e as batalhas acabaram resumidas a Quick Time Events (QTEs). Notei também alguns engasgos de performance; foram poucos (uns 4 ou 5 trechos no máximo), mas pontuais o suficiente para serem citados.

No geral, é uma releitura sólida, apesar de sacrificar parte da complexidade do clássico.
